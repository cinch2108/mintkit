Random Team Generator: Split a List into Fair Teams

How it works

Names are shuffled with an unbiased Fisher–Yates shuffle and then dealt out like cards, so team sizes never differ by more than one.

How to use it

  1. Paste the list of names.
  2. Choose the number of teams, or the number of people per team.
  3. Click Make teams, then copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

What if the names don't divide evenly?

Teams differ by at most one person. For example, 10 people in 3 teams gives teams of 4, 3 and 3.

Can I reshuffle?

Yes. Click Make teams again for a fresh, equally fair split.
