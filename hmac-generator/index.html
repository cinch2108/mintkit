HMAC Generator: HMAC-SHA256, SHA-512, SHA-1, MD5

How it works

HMAC combines a secret key with a hash function to produce a signature that proves a message wasn't altered and came from someone who knows the key. It's used to sign webhooks and APIs. Mintkit computes HMAC with Web Crypto (and its own HMAC-MD5) in your browser, so your key never leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Enter the message (or choose a file).
  2. Enter the secret key.
  3. Copy the HMAC in hex or Base64, or paste an expected signature to compare.

Frequently asked questions

How do I verify a webhook signature?

Paste the raw request body as the message and the webhook secret as the key, then compare the result with the signature header. Many services use HMAC-SHA256 in hex or Base64.

Is it safe to paste my secret key here?

The key is used only in your browser and is never sent or saved. As with any secret, avoid using it on shared computers.
