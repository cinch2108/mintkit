SMS QR Code Generator: Pre-filled Text Message

How it works

The code uses the SMSTO: format, which opens the phone's messaging app with your number and message ready to send. It's popular for text-to-join lists, contests and support lines.

How to use it

  1. Enter the phone number in international format, such as +1 555 010 1234.
  2. Type the message that should be pre-filled.
  3. Download and test-scan it with both an iPhone and an Android phone.

Frequently asked questions

Do people need an app to scan it?

No. Built-in camera apps on modern iPhones and Android phones recognize SMS QR codes.

Will they be charged for the text?

Standard messaging rates from their carrier apply, just as if they typed the message themselves.
