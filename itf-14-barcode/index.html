ITF-14 Barcode Generator for Shipping Cartons

How it works

ITF-14 encodes a 14-digit GTIN using Interleaved 2 of 5, where pairs of digits are coded in the bars and spaces together. Mintkit adds the GS1 check digit and the bearer bars (the thick frame) that stop partial scans on corrugated boxes.

How to use it

  1. Enter 13 digits to have the check digit added, or all 14 to verify it.
  2. Choose a larger bar width for printing on cardboard.
  3. Download the PNG or SVG.

Frequently asked questions

What are the thick bars around ITF-14 barcodes?

Bearer bars. They spread printing pressure on rough cardboard and prevent a scanner from reading only part of the code.

How does ITF-14 relate to EAN-13?

An ITF-14 is usually the product's EAN-13 with a packaging indicator digit in front and a new check digit at the end.
