QR Code Generator with Logo: Custom Colors, Free

How it works

Upload a logo and Mintkit places it in the center of the code on a padded background. Error correction is raised to High (30%) so the code can still be read even with the middle covered. Logos are checked to be real PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP images, resized and re-encoded in your browser, and never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter the link or text for your code.
  2. Click the logo picker and choose a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP image.
  3. Adjust the logo size (20% is a safe default) and your brand colors.
  4. Download as PNG or SVG and test-scan it before printing.

Frequently asked questions

How big can the logo be?

Up to about 25% of the code's width is usually safe with High error correction. Mintkit caps it at 28% and you should always test-scan the result.

Can I use brand colors?

Yes, but keep a dark foreground on a light background with strong contrast. Light-on-dark (inverted) codes fail on many scanners, and Mintkit warns you when contrast is too low.

Is my logo uploaded?

No. It's read, resized and redrawn entirely in your browser.
