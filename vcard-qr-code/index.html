vCard QR Code Generator for Business Cards

How it works

Your details are encoded as a vCard 3.0 contact card, the format phones use for contacts. When someone scans the code, their phone offers to save you as a new contact with your name, number, email, company, website and address.

How to use it

  1. Enter your name and the contact details you want to share.
  2. Leave any field you don't need empty to keep the code small.
  3. Download as SVG for printing on business cards, or PNG for email signatures and slides.

Frequently asked questions

Will the contact save automatically?

The phone shows a contact preview with an 'Add contact' or 'Create new contact' button, so people can save you in one tap.

Why does my vCard QR code look dense?

Every field adds data. Leave out fields you don't need, or print the code a little larger, so it scans easily.

Can I add a photo?

Photos make the code far too large to scan reliably, so the vCard holds text details only.
