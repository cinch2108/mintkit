Random PIN Generator: Secure 4 & 6 Digit PINs

How it works

Every digit is chosen with the Web Crypto API. The optional filter rejects common PINs such as 1234, 0000, repeated pairs and years from 1900 to 2099, which are the first ones attackers try.

How to use it

  1. Choose the number of digits.
  2. Keep 'Avoid easy PINs' switched on.
  3. Generate one or many, then copy.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 4-digit PIN secure?

It has only 10,000 possibilities, so it relies on lockouts after a few wrong attempts. Use 6 or more digits wherever you can.

Why avoid birth years?

Years like 1987 or 2001 are among the most guessed PINs because they're easy to find out.
