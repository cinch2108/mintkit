Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-25.

What you generate stays on your device

Every generator runs in your browser. We never receive, store or see the text, links, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, logos, files, passwords or hashes you create. Logos and files you pick are read locally and never uploaded. Preferences like your theme, password settings, wheel names and recently used tools are saved only in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time.

Advertising and cookies

Mintkit is free because of ads. If you accept cookies, our advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to show more relevant ads and measure them. If you choose "Only necessary," only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't use accounts, sign-ups or analytics that track you.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't upload anything you type, generate or choose in the tools.

Your rights

Under laws like the GDPR and CCPA, you can opt out of personalized ads at any time. Because we don't store your personal data, there's nothing for us to delete, but you can contact us at hello@example.com with any questions.
